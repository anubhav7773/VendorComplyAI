## 1. STRATEGIC MONETIZATION PHILOSOPHY (WHY CONSUMER ADS ARE BANNED)

In enterprise accounting and fintech platforms (where Managing Directors, CFOs, and Statutory Chartered Accountants manage crore-rupee ledgers), **consumer advertising (such as Google AdSense, banners, or programmatic popups) is strictly prohibited**. 

Introducing consumer ads causes catastrophic business failure:
1. **Destruction of Enterprise Trust**: Corporate promoters will immediately question the security and confidentiality of their financial books if they see consumer ads.
2. **Negligible Revenue**: AdSense pays ₹20 to ₹50 per 1,000 page views (CPM). A company running 5,000 page views a month generates less than ₹250/month in ad revenue.
3. **High Opportunity Cost**: A single B2B client subscription pays **₹78,000/year upfront**, and a single invoice financing referral pays **₹15,000 to ₹35,000 in pure cash commission**.

### The Three-Pillar Native Revenue Model:
Instead of consumer ads, VendorComply AI implements a **high-trust enterprise revenue triad**:
1. **Core SaaS Annual Subscriptions (Mandatory Upfront Billing)**: Provides upfront cash flow that covers all infrastructure costs for the entire year and guarantees founder personal runway.
2. **Embedded Working Capital & TReDS Financing Rails**: Contextual financing recommendations on the payout screen that earn a 0.5% to 1.0% origination bounty on disbursed credit.
3. **Bank Corporate Current Account Onboarding Bounty**: In-app partnership referrals for ICICI CIB and HDFC ENet opening bounties (₹3,000 to ₹8,000 per corporate onboarding).

from fastapi import HTTPException, status
from typing import Literal
from app.core.security import AuthenticatedUser


class EntitlementEnforcer:
    """
    Guarantees that high-value features (Connected Banking CSV export,
    Form 3CD certified exports, WhatsApp alerts) and monthly bill quotas
    are strictly gated based on the tenant's paid subscription tier.
    """

    @staticmethod
    def verify_bill_quota(tenant: dict):
        """
        Halts invoice ingestion if the monthly quota has been exceeded.
        """
        quota = tenant.get("monthly_bill_quota", 50)
        current_count = tenant.get("bills_processed_current_month", 0)

        if current_count >= quota:
            raise HTTPException(
                status_code=status.HTTP_402_PAYMENT_REQUIRED,
                detail={
                    "error_code": "BILL_QUOTA_EXCEEDED",
                    "message": f"Monthly invoice quota reached ({current_count}/{quota}). Upgrade to Growth Tier (₹6,500/mo) for up to 1,000 bills/month.",
                    "upgrade_url": "/settings/billing"
                }
            )

    @staticmethod
    def verify_feature_access(
        tenant: dict, 
        feature: Literal["BANK_CSV_EXPORT", "FORM_3CD_PACK", "WHATSAPP_ALERTS"]
    ):
        """
        Enforces feature gating on API endpoints.
        """
        tier = tenant.get("subscription_tier", "EVALUATION_TRIAL")
        status_billing = tenant.get("billing_status", "TRIAL_PERIOD")

        if status_billing == "EXPIRED":
            raise HTTPException(
                status_code=status.HTTP_402_PAYMENT_REQUIRED,
